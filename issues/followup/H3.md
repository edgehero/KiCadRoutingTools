**What happens**

A connector needs room for its plug, and the plug needs room for fingers. A small part needs room around it to be soldered, inspected and reworked. Placement models neither:
- courtyards stop at the body, and check_assembly gates courtyard overlaps only;
- an edge connector has an overhang band, and a `cable_envelope_mm` that applies to file-locked parts only;
- a vertical header in the middle of the board has no mating envelope at all.

- The results are the same on two runs.
- `kicad_files/kit-dev-coldfire-xilinx_5213` the same way: jammed goes 6 → 12.
- A single all-parts group keeps the test mechanical. The real skill writes groups by hand, and the gpm runs below used them.

The two measures are:
- **plug ring:** a part taller than a vertical connector within 1.5 mm of its body;
- **jammed:** a part up to 2 mm tall with parts at least 4 mm and 3× its height on two opposite sides, each closer than half its own height.

Both are deliberately simple first cuts.

**The same in the gpm campaign** (placed by the skill, with its groups; for context)
- **ecc83-pp_v2: plug space.** The 2-pin headers P1, P3 and P4 (8.6 mm) sit 0.29 mm from the ECC83 valve (50 mm tall) and 1.3 mm from the 12.5 mm electrolytic C1. A 2-pin housing is wider than its header, so no plug fits and no finger reaches it. The human board has none like that, and the board graded DONE.
- **icebreaker_v10e: SMD parts jammed between tall parts.** Two 0402 resistor arrays, a 0603 and a SOT-23-5 each sit 1.1-1.9 mm between 5.2-8.6 mm headers and sockets on two opposite sides. That is 5 parts, against 2 on the human board. No iron, 45° AOI camera or rework nozzle reaches a part shadowed like that, and the headers' own plugs need the space.
- **complex_hierarchy: through-hole parts jammed.** 11 axial resistors sit between TO-92s, trimpots and terminal blocks, against 5 on the human board. They are soldered from the far side, so this matters less, but the trimpots need screwdriver room.

**Expected**
1. **A mating envelope per connector,** from a class default with a declared override. For a vertical connector it is the plug housing over the body plus a margin, up to the plug's height. For a right-angle one it is the plug's length out of the mating face. The envelope is priced in the anneal and graded, as courtyards are.
2. **An access rule:** a part with tall neighbours on opposite sides closer than k times their height is reported. Whether it is advisory or an error depends on the board's assembly, hand versus reflow with AOI, which the brief could declare.
3. Both need part heights (#1443).
